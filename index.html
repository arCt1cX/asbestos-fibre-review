Should this be counted as a fibre?

Count it if it is longer than 5 µm, thinner than 3 µm and at least 3 times longer than wide.
  • Don't count it with an attached particle larger than 3 µm, or if it is a tangle or a compact bundle.
  • Crossing fibres: each one counts. A split fibre counts as 1.
  • The bar in the magnified view = 10 µm. Hold the space bar to hide the box and the circle.
  • If in doubt: Not sure. You can go back and change any answer.
